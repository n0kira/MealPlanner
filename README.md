# What's Fresh?

Having to decide what to eat it's always hard, that's why What's Fresh? exixsts!

Plan your weekly meals ahead of time and stop wasting moments of your life, I got you covered! Just select all the meals for the week or day!

## How does it work?

That's pretty easy!
Want to select what you are going to eat today? Just select your meals in the home page. You can search for various meals and use the filters to include only what you're feeling like eating.
Want to plan the whole week? Just select the weekly planner on the menu and choose meals for each day!
All of the selected meals are saved locally and each day the daily planner will sync with the meals planned for the week.
